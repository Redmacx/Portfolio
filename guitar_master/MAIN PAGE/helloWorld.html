Master of Puppets

Released March 3, 1986, this thrash metal masterpiece from Metallica's third studio album features one of the most iconic opening riffs in rock history. Tuned to standard E, the song runs at a ferocious 212 BPM and showcases James Hetfield's legendary rhythm guitar work alongside Kirk Hammett's scorching leads.
